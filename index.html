The favorite with a minus sign, and how many points it's favored by. If the line moved afterward, a smaller "now" or "closed" line shows where it went. The pick and result always use the Market line.

Under the hood

How it works

Opponent-adjusted efficiency

Before every week, each team's offense and defense are re-rated from scratch on points, EPA per play, success rate, explosiveness, rushing and passing, down and distance, line yards, scoring chances, field position, turnovers and kicking. A 30-point game against a great defense counts for more than one against a weak one.

Roster and coaching

247 talent composite, the last four recruiting classes, returning production, transfer portal gains and first-year or interim head coaches. These matter most early in the season and fade as results pile up.

Injuries

Official Big 12, ACC and SEC availability reports are read automatically before each game day. Players missing from recent box scores are tracked for everyone else.

Situation

Rest and bye weeks, travel distance and time zones, altitude, rivalry and revenge spots, lookahead and letdown games, bowls and title games, and kickoff weather for totals.

No peeking

Every rating and model is trained only on games played before the week it predicts, and the betting line is never an input. A new model only replaces the current one if it wins on games it has never seen.

For entertainment and research only. Nothing here is betting advice; the model has not shown an edge over closing lines.
